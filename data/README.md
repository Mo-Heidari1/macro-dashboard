# Data

ISM data is read only from the committed files in `data/sample/`. The
Treasury loader reads `data/local/<file>` if it exists, otherwise
`data/sample/<file>` (`src/core/paths.py`). All stored data is CSV.
Workbooks and ISM industry comment text are never committed.

| File | Folder | Source | Licence | Frequency | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `yields_history.csv` | `sample/` (committed, full history) | U.S. Department of the Treasury, daily par yield curve rates (nominal and real); FRED series EFFR (effective Fed Funds rate) | Public | Weekly (Sunday-dated, carrying the prior Friday's close) to 2025; daily from 2026-01-01 | Refreshed with `scripts/update_yields.py`. Validated cell by cell against Treasury.gov for 1990–2026 (rows added through 2026-10-21 also checked against FRED for Fed Funds) with `scripts/validate_yields.py` (nominal and TIPS; Fed Funds not covered). Corrected from Treasury.gov: 2026 weekday TIPS (were missing), the 3mo column on Sunday rows 2021-12-26 to 2026-06-28 (held a lower, T-bill-style rate), and the stale 2021-01-03 row. |
| `ism_history.csv` | `sample/` (committed) | headline PMI and 10 sub-indices. Source: ISM Manufacturing Report On Business (Institute for Supply Management). 1948–Sep 2026: ISM monthly releases (ismworld.org), with historical data via Investing.com and Quandl. | Committed with attribution (licence code A) | Monthly, 1948 to Sep 2026 (sub-index start dates vary) | Updated by hand each month with `scripts/update_ism.py` or the page form, or imported from the local workbook with `scripts/extract_ism.py --months`. Validated against the workbook's cached values with `scripts/validate_ism.py` (all months, all 11 series). |
| `ism_sectors.csv` | `sample/` (committed) | industries reporting growth/contraction, ranked. Source: ISM Manufacturing Report On Business (Institute for Supply Management). 1948–Sep 2026: ISM monthly releases (ismworld.org), with historical data via Investing.com and Quandl. | Committed with attribution (licence code A) | Monthly, Apr 2026 to Sep 2026 (all 7 categories) | Long format: Category, Date, Industry, Direction, Score (rank within growth/contraction; 0 = neutral). Only rows from `SECTORS_START` (2026-04) are committed. Industry comment text is never committed. |
| `ISM_Manufacturing_Index_Updated2.xlsx` | `local/` (git-ignored) | Source workbook for the ISM CSVs (through Sep 2026; replaces `ISM_Manufacturing_Index_Updated.xlsx`) | Never committed | — | Workbooks and industry comment text stay local. The old local `ism_history.csv` / `ism_sectors.csv` copies here are no longer read. |
